
# Lesson 2: Talking about yourself

## Dialog 1

*mi nim es Tomas.*
– My name is Thomas.  
*mi have 25 (due ten faif) yer.*
– I am 25 years old.  
*mi es studer e sporter.*
– I am a student and a sportsman.  
*mi stude historia.*
– I study history.  
*mi sporte futebol.*
– I play football.  
*mi have pa, ma, un brat e du sis en mi familia.*
– I have father, mother, a brother and two sisters in my family.

## Dialog 2

<big>🧓</big>
*wa es tu nim?*
– What's your name?  
<big>👩</big>
*mi nim es Maria.*
– My name is Maria.  
<big>🧓</big>
*tu have wa multi yer?*
– How old are you?  
<big>👩</big>
*mi have 30 (tri ten) yer.*
– I am 30 years old.  
<big>🧓</big>
*tu du wa profesie?*
– What do you do by profession?  
<big>👩</big>
*mi es un ticher.*
– I am a teacher.  
<big>🧓</big>
*tu tiche wa ting?*
– What do you teach?  
<big>👩</big>
*mi tiche matematike en mide skole.*
– I teach mathematics in the secondary school.  
<big>🧓</big>
*tu du wa hobi?*
– What do you do as a hobby?  
<big>👩</big>
*mi stude linguaje.*
– I study languages.
